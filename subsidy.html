GOVERNMENT OF INDIA SCHEME

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Complete guide to government solar subsidies in Bihar. Direct bank transfer up to ₹78,000 for residential rooftops.

GOVERNMENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

How Much Subsidy Will You Receive?

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, the Government of India credits fixed financial aid directly into your bank account post installation and net-metering setup.

1kW
1 kW Solar System: ₹30,000 Direct Subsidy Ideal for small homes with basic light & fan load
2kW
2 kW Solar System: ₹60,000 Direct Subsidy Suitable for medium homes running refrigerator & washing machine
3kW+
3 kW to 10 kW System: ₹78,000 Maximum Subsidy Recommended for homes running 1.5 Ton AC units & water pumps

Documents Needed For Subsidy

  • Latest Electricity Bill (NBPDCL / SBPDCL)
  • Applicant's Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook / Cancelled Cheque
  • Rooftop Clear Photo
