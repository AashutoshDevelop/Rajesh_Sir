About Greeja Organic Solar

Pioneering clean energy independence and PM Surya Ghar yojana subsidy execution across North Bihar.

OUR FOUNDATION

Our Story & Core Vision

Founded by Dr. Rajesh Kumar, Greeja Organic Solar Energy Pvt. Ltd. was built on a simple philosophy: integrating clean solar energy with organic sustainability. As an official channel partner for UTL Solar, we bridge the energy gap in Muzaffarpur, providing residential and commercial clients with robust, uninterrupted clean power.

Our Mission

To accelerate clean energy transition across Bihar by offering world-class solar engineering, hassle-free government subsidy approvals, and 25-year performance security.

UTL Solar Official Partnership

Authorized Channel Partner

As an authorized UTL Solar partner, we supply MNRE/ALMM certified solar panels, rMPPT hybrid inverters, and C10 deep-cycle batteries. Every rooftop installation carries official UTL warranties.
